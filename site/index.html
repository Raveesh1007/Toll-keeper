Can NEAR's tolls pay for its road?

NEAR keeps a small toll on every cross-chain swap. It also prints new tokens to pay for its security. If the tolls grow to cover that cost, NEAR holders win. This page checks that every day from public data.

Words used here

NEAR Intents
NEAR's service that swaps tokens between blockchains, e.g. Bitcoin to Ethereum. Each swap pays a small fee.
Revenue
The part of those fees NEAR keeps. Since Feb 2026 it buys NEAR tokens on the market.
Security cost
New NEAR printed each year to pay validators. It dilutes holders, so it is a real cost.
Coverage
Revenue ÷ security cost. At 100%, buybacks cancel out the printing.
Signing network
Independent operators who jointly hold the keys that move assets across chains. "11 of 16" = any 11 must agree.
Staked
Share of all NEAR locked up to secure the network.
